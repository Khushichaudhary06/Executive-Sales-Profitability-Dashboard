# 📊 Executive Sales & Profitability Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

The **Executive Sales & Profitability Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides a comprehensive analysis of sales performance, revenue, profitability, product categories, sales channels, and geographical distribution.

The main objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Monitor gross profit and gross margin.
- Identify monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Compare top-performing and low-performing countries.
- Analyze product category performance.
- Evaluate revenue contribution by sales channel.
- Understand geographical sales distribution.
- Build an interactive business dashboard.

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Measures overall sales revenue |
| Total Gross Profit | Measures profit generated from sales |
| Gross Margin | Shows gross profit as a percentage of revenue |
| Total Quantity | Represents the total quantity of products sold |
| Total Orders | Shows the number of unique orders |

---

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Monthly Revenue & Profit Trend
- Analyzes monthly revenue and gross profit.
- Identifies sales and profitability trends.
- Helps understand monthly business performance.

### 2. Top 5 Countries by Revenue
- Identifies the five highest-revenue countries.
- Highlights the strongest geographical markets.

### 3. Bottom 5 Countries by Revenue
- Identifies the five lowest-revenue countries.
- Helps recognize markets that may require improvement.

### 4. Geographical Sales Distribution
- Displays sales performance across countries.
- Provides a geographical perspective on revenue distribution.

### 5. Revenue by Sales Channel
- Compares revenue generated through different sales channels.
- Shows the contribution of each channel.

### 6. Revenue by Product Category
- Analyzes revenue generated by different product categories.
- Helps identify important product segments.

## DAX Measures

Total Revenue = SUM(SalesData[Sales])

Total Gross Profit =
SUM(SalesData[Profit])

Gross Margin =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Gross Profit],
    [Total Revenue],
    0
)

Total Quantity =
SUM(SalesData[Order Quantity])

Total Orders =
DISTINCTCOUNT(SalesData[Order ID])
